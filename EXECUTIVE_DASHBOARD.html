₹10 Cr Investment Recommendation

→ Better Borrower Targeting
Attempt volume/account is already flat and not the constraint — the constraint is conversion per contact on a book whose real recovery is falling despite unchanged effort. Re-prioritizing existing contact budget toward higher-probability-of-payment accounts (DPD, risk_segment, payment history) is the lowest-risk lever available. Confidence: low-to-moderate — no cost table exists in this dataset, so ROI/break-even cannot be point-estimated; recommend a funded 60–90 day targeting pilot before committing the full ₹10 Cr.
